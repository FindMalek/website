### StageUp: Connecting Students with Internship Opportunities

**StageUp** is a innovative platform designed to help students find and secure internships by providing real-time job listings and a seamless user experience. Developed as my final project of the year, this platform addresses the challenges students face when searching for internships, making the process more efficient and accessible.

#### **Project Inspiration**

The idea for **StageUp** came from recognizing the difficulties students encounter when looking for internships. Traditional job search methods can be time-consuming and inefficient. To solve this, we created a platform that aggregates internship listings in real-time, allowing students to easily find and apply for opportunities that match their skills and interests.

#### **Features**

- **Real-Time Job Listings:**

  - Utilizing automated scrapers, the platform continuously updates its database with the latest internship opportunities, ensuring that students have access to the most current listings.

- **User-Friendly Interface:**

  - The platform features an intuitive and easy-to-navigate interface, allowing students to search, filter ([`InternshipFilter`](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/components/sections/internships/InternshipFilter.tsx)), and apply for internships with ease. The list page reads from the [internships API](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/app/api/internship/route.ts#L130-L160) through [`getInternships`](<https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/app/(authed)/internships/page.tsx#L9-L25>).
  - Students can create profiles, upload resumes ([`uploadFile`](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/lib/storage.ts#L4-L15) to Supabase Storage, called from the [intern profile form](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/components/sections/forms/Intern.tsx#L183-L205)), and manage their applications all in one place.

- **Seamless Application Process:**

  - The platform streamlines the application process, enabling students to apply for internships directly through the platform.
  - Companies can review applications, communicate with candidates, and manage the hiring process efficiently ([enterprise applications table](<https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/app/(authed)/applications/page.tsx>), [`DataTable`](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/components/sections/application/enterprise/DataTable.tsx), [new internship dialog](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/components/sections/application/enterprise/DialogNewInternship.tsx)).

- **Comprehensive Dashboard:**
  - Students can track the status of their applications, receive notifications, and stay updated on new opportunities that match their preferences.

#### **Technical Implementation**

The platform is built using a modern tech stack to ensure performance, scalability, and security:

- **Frontend:**
  - **React and Next.js:** For building dynamic and responsive user interfaces ([Next.js 14](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/package.json#L38)).
  - **TailwindCSS:** For creating a sleek and customizable design ([config](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/tailwind.config.ts)).
- **Backend:**
  - **TypeScript:** For robust and type-safe code.
  - **Prisma:** For efficient database management ([`Intern`, `Enterprise` and `Internship` models](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/prisma/schema.prisma#L59-L122)).
  - **NextAuth:** For secure authentication ([GitHub, Google and credentials providers](https://github.com/FindMalek/StageUp/blob/95086d4/lib/auth.ts#L20-L34)).
- **Hosting and Services:**
  - **Vercel:** For hosting and performance monitoring.
  - **GitHub (Codebase, Issues, PRs, Actions):** For version control and collaboration.
  - **Automated Scrapers:** For aggregating real-time job listings from various sources.
