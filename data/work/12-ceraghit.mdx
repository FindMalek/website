### Ceraghit Creation: an online shop for a ceramics studio

Ceraghit Creation is the studio of Sabri Ghit, who models terracotta sculptures and objects by hand in Tunisia. He needed to sell to people in Tunisia and abroad and to run it all from his phone. I built the whole thing as a freelancer, alone, between October 22 and November 27, 2026: 263 commits and 252 merged pull requests.

- A storefront in French, English and Arabic (right to left), with two prices per piece, dinars and euros, both set by the owner.
- Cash on delivery in Tunisia and a bank transfer abroad, confirmed by the owner in an audited step. No card gateway in production.
- An owner dashboard made for a phone: orders, products, a media library that compresses photos to WebP before upload, invoices, CSV export, payment and shipping settings.
- An oRPC api with a REST mirror and OpenAPI docs, scheduled jobs, rate limits, encrypted provider secrets and end to end security tests.
- Production on Vercel, Neon and Cloudflare R2, staging and a full copy per pull request on my home lab, with the demo login posted on the PR.

The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/FindMalek/ceraghit). The full write-up, with screenshots, is the [case study](/projects/ceraghit).
