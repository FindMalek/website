### Endless Byte: Client Work as a Student-Run Agency

I founded **Endless Byte** with two university classmates, **Hamza Haj Mtir** and **Iheb el Azhari**, as a small software agency delivering client projects while we were studying — a way to gain practical experience and generate income alongside our degrees.

#### **Client: Promolab — Custom E-commerce Platform**

Our first major client project was for **Promolab**, a company serving major accounts including STEG-affiliated business in Tunisia. I designed and built a custom e-commerce website using **ReactJS** and **Next.js**.

- **Responsive Design:** Built with ReactJS and Next.js for a responsive, user-friendly shopping experience across desktop and mobile.
- **Database Management:** Implemented **Supabase** and **Prisma** to handle database operations, ensuring data integrity and performance at scale.
- **Product Management Dashboard:** Built a comprehensive dashboard enabling easy addition, editing, and deletion of items, so administrators could keep the catalog current without engineering involvement.

#### **Client: Gym Chain CRM**

We also delivered a membership-management CRM for gym chains, including **California Gym**, covering subscription management across multiple locations nationwide.

- **Subscription Management:** Centralized member subscription tracking across every location, replacing manual/per-location record keeping.
- **WhatsApp Promotions:** Built WhatsApp-based promotional messaging to reach members directly with offers and updates.

#### **Team and Collaboration**

- Worked closely with co-founders **Hamza Haj Mtir** and **Iheb el Azhari** across both client engagements, from technical architecture to delivery.
- This work provided real-world experience in web development, database management, and client delivery — running an agency, not just writing code, while still full-time students.
