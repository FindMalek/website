### Undrstnd Labs: Building an Inference API After Y Combinator Said No

I co-founded **Undrstnd Labs** with **Denis Cartin**. We applied to Y Combinator — and got rejected. Rather than treat that as the end of the road, we launched the company independently in Italy instead.

#### **The Product**

Undrstnd Labs offers inference that's meaningfully faster and cheaper than OpenAI, running on bigger and better models — the core pitch being real cost and performance advantages, not just an alternative for its own sake.

- **Fast, Cheap Inference:** Built infrastructure to serve inference significantly cheaper than OpenAI, without giving up model quality.
- **Bigger, Better Models:** Offered access to larger, stronger models than typical cost-focused alternatives.
- **Developer-First API:** Designed the API around developer experience, with **Vercel AI SDK** compatibility so teams already building on that stack could adopt it with minimal friction.

#### **From Rejection to Launch**

Getting rejected from Y Combinator didn't end the company — it just meant building it a different way, self-funded and based in Italy instead of through the accelerator track. This period covers building the core product and infrastructure before the transition to CTO as the company matured (see the following role).

---

### Impact and Achievements

- **Resilience:** Turned a Y Combinator rejection into an independently launched, running company rather than shelving the idea.
- **Product Positioning:** Built a genuinely differentiated offering — faster and cheaper than OpenAI on stronger models, not a like-for-like clone.
- **Ecosystem Fit:** Made the API a drop-in option for the existing Vercel AI SDK developer base rather than requiring a bespoke integration.
